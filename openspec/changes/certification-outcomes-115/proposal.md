## Why

Learners need to record actual certification outcomes independently of practice and mock exam scores. Refs #115.

## What changes

- Add a private outcome journal with optional study roadmap links.
- Add explicit preview and moderated sharing of a separate public projection.
- Add a role-gated moderation queue and a separate public feed.

## Capabilities

- `certification-outcomes`

## Impact

Profile and Exam History navigation, candidate Firestore Rules, and two opt-in frontend flags.
