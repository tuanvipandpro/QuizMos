## Why

Learners need a private study schedule tied to a certification and an exam target. Refs #116.

## What changes

- Add a signed-in Study roadmap with up to three plans, each with a public quiz bank, exam date, time zone and weekly session goal.
- Generate weekly tasks with quiz links, optional Exam History evidence and explicit manual completion and rescheduling.
- Add owner-scoped storage and staged rollout verification. The #85 due queue can attach to these calendar dates once available.

## Capabilities

- `study-roadmap`

## Impact

Account navigation, Firestore Rules, the public quiz catalog, and read-only Exam History integration.
