## Why

Learners need predictable, touch-friendly settings for Custom timed training. Refs #187 and #82.

## What changes

- Offer independent 50, 60, 70 and 80 presets for question count and duration.
- Explain disabled count presets based on the eligible pool.
- Explicitly use all eligible questions when a nonempty pool is smaller than the first preset.

## Impact

Timed session setup in quiz overview. Certification format, saved drafts and Practice keep their existing contracts.
